# Engineering change request ECR-0125: Inner door pane coating

## Problem

During pyrolysis, the outer door glass of OV-600P rises by 63.4 K, above the 60.0 K limit
(TR-2026-052).

## Proposed change

Add a reflective low-emissivity coating to the inner door pane. Thermal simulation predicts a
reduction of 5 to 7 K during pyrolysis.

## Cost and timing

Part cost +1.40 EUR per unit. Supplier samples are expected in October 2026. A retest is needed
before approval.

## Risk

The coating must withstand 480 °C. The supplier has qualified the coating up to 500 °C.
