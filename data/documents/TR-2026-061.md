# Test report TR-2026-061: Door hinge durability

## Scope

Open-close cycling of the door at room temperature according to SPEC-OV600-001 section 3.5. The
test stops at 30,000 cycles or at the first failure.

## Results

| Variant | Door mass (kg) | Cycles reached | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OV-600B | 9.8 | 30000 | no failure |
| OV-600P | 11.2 | 30000 | no failure |
| OV-600S | 12.6 | 26500 | hinge spring fracture |

## Findings

OV-600S fails the 30,000 cycle requirement. The steam variant has the heaviest door because of its
extra seal frame, and the hinge spring is the same part as on the lighter variants. The fracture
started at the spring hook.

## Recommendation

Use a spring wire with higher fatigue strength for OV-600S and repeat the test.
