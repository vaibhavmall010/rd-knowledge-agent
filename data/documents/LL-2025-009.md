# Lessons learned LL-2025-009: Fan tone on OV-500

## What happened

The previous platform OV-500 showed an audible fan tone at launch. The blade-pass frequency of the
equal-spaced fan coincided with an acoustic mode of the cavity. Field complaints followed in the
first three months, and the fix arrived late because it needed new fan tooling.

## Root cause

Cavity acoustic modes were not checked against the fan blade-pass frequency during design review.

## Recommendations

- Calculate the blade-pass frequency for every fan speed and compare it with the cavity modes in
  the design review.
- Prefer unequal blade spacing. It spread the tone on OV-500 without changing air flow.
- Measure tonal components, not only the overall dB(A) level.
