# Engineering change request ECR-0123: Descaling counter

## Problem

TR-2026-058 showed a 15 percent loss of steam output after 120 cycles with hard water, while the
descaling alert only comes after a fixed 200 cycles.

## Proposed change

Replace the fixed counter with a counter that weights each cycle by the water hardness setting and
by the measured heating time of the steam generator. Longer heating time indicates scale build-up.

## Open points

- Validation plan with three water hardness levels is still missing.
- The requirement of 150 cycles at 3.5 mmol/l cannot be met by software alone. A larger generator
  or a scale filter must be evaluated, or the requirement must be reviewed.

## Timing

Target implementation in the software release of Q4 2026.
