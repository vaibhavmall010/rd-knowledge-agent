# Engineering change request ECR-0117: Fan blade change

## Problem

TR-2026-040 measured 54.1 dB(A) on OV-600P with a clearly audible tone at 216 Hz, above the 52.0
dB(A) limit. The tone is the blade-pass frequency of the 7-blade fan at 1850 rpm.

## Change

Replace the 7-blade fan with a 9-blade fan with unequal blade spacing. Unequal spacing spreads the
tonal energy over several frequencies. The new part is used on OV-600B and OV-600P. OV-600S keeps
its own fan because of the steam generator layout.

## Cost and timing

Tooling cost 18,000 EUR, part cost +0.12 EUR per unit. Implemented in the B-sample build of May 2026.

## Verification

Verified by TR-2026-047: OV-600P at 51.2 dB(A), no tonal component.
