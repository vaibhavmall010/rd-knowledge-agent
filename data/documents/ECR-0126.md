# Engineering change request ECR-0126: Hinge spring for OV-600S

## Problem

The OV-600S hinge spring fractured at 26,500 cycles in TR-2026-061, below the 30,000 cycle
requirement. The OV-600S door is 12.6 kg, the heaviest of the platform.

## Change

Use a shot-peened spring of oil-tempered SiCr wire for OV-600S only. OV-600B and OV-600P keep the
current spring.

## Cost and timing

Part cost +0.35 EUR per unit. Approved on 2026-07-30. Parts for the retest arrive in September 2026,
and the durability retest is planned for October 2026.
