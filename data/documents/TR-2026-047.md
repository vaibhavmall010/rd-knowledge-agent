# Test report TR-2026-047: Fan noise retest after ECR-0117

## Scope

Retest of fan noise with the 9-blade fan with unequal blade spacing introduced by ECR-0117.
OV-600B was measured as well because it uses the same fan.

## Results

| Variant | Sound pressure level (dB(A)) | Tonal component |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OV-600B | 49.8 | none |
| OV-600P | 51.2 | none |

## Findings

OV-600P now meets the 52.0 dB(A) limit with a margin of 0.8 dB(A), and the 216 Hz tone is no longer
audible. OV-600B improved by 1.1 dB(A). Air flow and temperature uniformity were spot-checked on
OV-600P and did not change.

## Conclusion

ECR-0117 is verified. The fan noise topic for OV-600P is closed.
