# Test report TR-2026-052: Door surface temperature

## Scope

Temperature rise of the outer door glass above ambient according to SPEC-OV600-001 section 3.4,
at 200 °C in conventional mode for all variants and during the pyrolysis cycle for OV-600P.

## Results

| Variant | Mode | Temperature rise (K) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OV-600B | 200 °C conventional | 38.5 |
| OV-600P | 200 °C conventional | 41.0 |
| OV-600S | 200 °C conventional | 39.7 |
| OV-600P | Pyrolysis, 480 °C | 63.4 |

## Findings

All variants meet the 45.0 K limit in normal operation. During pyrolysis, OV-600P exceeds the
60.0 K limit by 3.4 K. The hottest spot is the upper centre of the outer glass. The door has three
glass panes and only the middle pane has a reflective coating.

## Recommendation

Evaluate a reflective low-emissivity coating on the inner pane. Thermal simulation predicts a
reduction of 5 to 7 K.
