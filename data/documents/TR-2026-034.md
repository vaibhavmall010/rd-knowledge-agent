# Test report TR-2026-034: Energy consumption

## Scope

Energy consumption per cycle was measured with the brick method of EN 60350-1 on one B-sample of
each variant, in conventional and fan-forced mode.

## Results

| Variant | Conventional (kWh) | Fan-forced (kWh) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OV-600B | 0.98 | 0.79 |
| OV-600P | 1.01 | 0.82 |
| OV-600S | 1.04 | 0.88 |

## Findings

All variants meet the conventional limit of 1.05 kWh. OV-600S exceeds the fan-forced limit of
0.85 kWh by 0.03 kWh. The extra mass of the steam generator housing heats up with the cavity, and
the poor air flow found in TR-2026-031 lengthens the heating phases.

## Recommendation

Repeat the fan-forced measurement on OV-600S after the fan baffle change. No action for OV-600B and
OV-600P.
