# Product Requirements Specification, OV-600 Built-in Oven Platform

Fictional example data for a portfolio project. The OV-600 platform has three variants:
OV-600B (standard built-in oven), OV-600P (pyrolytic self-cleaning) and OV-600S (steam combi oven).
Limits in this document are the internal release criteria. Every variant must meet them before the
platform release gate.

## 3.1 Cavity temperature and preheating

The cavity temperature deviation is measured at nine points in an empty cavity at a 180 °C setpoint
in fan-forced mode. The maximum deviation from the cavity mean must not exceed 10.0 K.

Preheating from room temperature to 200 °C in fan-forced mode must take no longer than 7.5 minutes.

## 3.2 Energy consumption

Energy consumption is measured with the brick method according to EN 60350-1.
The limits per cycle are:

- Conventional heating: at most 1.05 kWh per cycle.
- Fan-forced heating: at most 0.85 kWh per cycle.

These limits keep every variant within energy efficiency class A+ with a margin.

## 3.3 Acoustics

Fan noise is measured as A-weighted sound pressure level at 1 m in front of the door in fan-forced
mode at 200 °C. The limit is 52.0 dB(A). Tonal components that are clearly audible must be reported
even when the overall level is within the limit.

## 3.4 Door surface temperature

The temperature rise of the outer door glass above ambient is measured at 200 °C in conventional
mode after 60 minutes. The limit is 45.0 K for all variants.

For the pyrolytic variant OV-600P, the door temperature rise during the pyrolysis cleaning cycle
(480 °C cavity) must not exceed 60.0 K.

## 3.5 Mechanical durability

The door hinge must survive at least 30,000 open-close cycles without failure or a change in door
alignment of more than 1.5 mm.

## 3.6 Steam generator (OV-600S only)

With water of hardness 3.5 mmol/l, the steam generator must run at least 150 steam cycles before a
descaling alert is required. Scale build-up must not reduce steam output by more than 15 percent
before the alert.
