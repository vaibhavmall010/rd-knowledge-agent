# Lessons learned LL-2025-014: Descaling counter on SC-300

## What happened

The SC-300 steam oven used a fixed cycle counter for the descaling alert. In regions with hard water
the steam output dropped well before the alert, and customers reported weak steam and white
deposits in the cavity.

## Root cause

The counter ignored water hardness. Testing was done only with medium-hard water of 2.0 mmol/l.

## Recommendations

- Test steam generators at 3.5 mmol/l or harder.
- Base the descaling alert on water hardness and on measured heating time.
