# Test report TR-2026-066: OV-600S retest after ECR-0121

## Scope

Retest of OV-600S with the redesigned rear fan baffle from ECR-0121, covering temperature
uniformity, preheat time and fan-forced energy consumption.

## Results

| Parameter | Before (TR-2026-031 / TR-2026-034) | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max deviation from mean (K) | 11.2 | 9.1 |
| Preheat to 200 °C (min) | 8.1 | 7.6 |
| Fan-forced energy (kWh) | 0.88 | 0.84 |

## Findings

Temperature uniformity and fan-forced energy now meet the specification. Preheat time improved by
0.5 minutes but is still 0.1 minutes above the 7.5 minute limit. The remaining gap comes from the
thermal mass of the steam generator housing, not from air flow.

## Recommendation

Either raise the preheat heater power for OV-600S or request a deviation for the preheat
requirement. This is open, see the platform review minutes MEET-2026-09-10.
