# Minutes: OV-600 platform review, 10 September 2026

Participants: project management, thermal, acoustics, mechanics, software, quality.

## Status per variant

- OV-600B: all requirements met. Ready for the release gate.
- OV-600P: fan noise closed with ECR-0117. Door temperature during pyrolysis still open, waiting for
  ECR-0125 samples.
- OV-600S: uniformity and energy closed with ECR-0121. Open: preheat time (7.6 min against 7.5 min),
  hinge durability (ECR-0126) and descaling (ECR-0123).

## Decisions

1. The platform release gate moves from 2026-10-15 to 2026-11-30.
2. OV-600B may pass the release gate on its own if the other variants are not ready.
3. For the OV-600S preheat time, quality will request a deviation to 7.7 minutes instead of raising
   heater power, because a higher power would need a new EMC test.
