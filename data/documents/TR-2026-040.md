# Test report TR-2026-040: Fan noise

## Scope

A-weighted sound pressure level at 1 m in front of the door, fan-forced mode at 200 °C, according to
SPEC-OV600-001 section 3.3.

## Results

| Variant | Sound pressure level (dB(A)) | Tonal component |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OV-600B | 50.9 | none |
| OV-600P | 54.1 | 216 Hz, clearly audible |
| OV-600S | 51.6 | none |

## Findings

OV-600P exceeds the 52.0 dB(A) limit by 2.1 dB(A). The tonal component at 216 Hz matches the
blade-pass frequency of the 7-blade fan at 1850 rpm. The thicker insulation of the pyrolytic cavity
shifts an acoustic cavity mode close to this frequency, so the tone is amplified. The same effect
was seen on the previous platform, see LL-2025-009.

## Recommendation

Change the fan blade count or spacing so the blade-pass frequency moves away from the cavity mode.
