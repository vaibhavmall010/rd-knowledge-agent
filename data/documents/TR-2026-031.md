# Test report TR-2026-031: Cavity temperature uniformity and preheat time

## Scope

Temperature uniformity and preheat time were measured on one B-sample of each variant according to
SPEC-OV600-001 section 3.1.

## Results

| Variant | Max deviation from mean (K) | Preheat to 200 °C (min) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OV-600B | 7.8 | 6.9 |
| OV-600P | 8.4 | 7.1 |
| OV-600S | 11.2 | 8.1 |

## Findings

OV-600B and OV-600P meet both requirements. OV-600S fails the uniformity limit of 10.0 K and the
preheat limit of 7.5 minutes. The hottest point is the rear upper corner next to the steam inlet.
Smoke tests show that the steam generator housing blocks part of the rear fan outlet, so air flow
to the lower front of the cavity is weak.

## Recommendation

Redesign the rear fan baffle of OV-600S to restore air flow around the steam generator housing,
then repeat the test.
