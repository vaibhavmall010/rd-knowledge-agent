# Engineering change request ECR-0121: Rear fan baffle for OV-600S

## Problem

OV-600S fails temperature uniformity (11.2 K against 10.0 K), preheat time (8.1 min against 7.5
min) and fan-forced energy (0.88 kWh against 0.85 kWh), see TR-2026-031 and TR-2026-034. The steam
generator housing blocks part of the rear fan outlet.

## Change

New rear baffle with two additional air outlets at the lower corners and a deflector around the
steam generator housing.

## Cost and timing

Tooling change 6,500 EUR, no change in part cost. Implemented on B-samples in July 2026.

## Verification

Verified by TR-2026-066. Uniformity and energy now pass. Preheat time is 7.6 min and still fails by
0.1 min, so a follow-up decision is needed.
