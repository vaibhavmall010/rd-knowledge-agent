# Test report TR-2026-058: Steam generator scaling

## Scope

Endurance test of the OV-600S steam generator with water of hardness 3.5 mmol/l according to
SPEC-OV600-001 section 3.6. Steam output was measured every 10 cycles.

## Results

Steam output fell by 15 percent after 120 steam cycles. The current software raises the descaling
alert after a fixed count of 200 cycles, independent of water hardness.

| Variant | Cycles until 15 % output loss | Alert raised at cycle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OV-600S | 120 | 200 |

## Findings

The requirement of at least 150 cycles before descaling is not met with hard water. The alert also
comes too late: users would run 80 cycles with reduced steam output. The fixed counter was also the
root cause of field complaints on the previous steam oven, see LL-2025-014.

## Recommendation

Make the descaling counter depend on the water hardness setting and on measured heating time.
